The twelve-worker grid implementation is retained for the qualified inputs listed below; fixed-window Stellar was subsequently approved by the user as an acceptable speed/footprint tradeoff, while its memory interval remains inconclusive. ## Worker budget and mechanism

`JavaPotRunner.trainModels` reserves `F=min(maxWorkers, foldCount)` outer workers and at most `maxWorkers−F` shared helpers on the opt-in path. Fold workers also evaluate pairs. There are no nested pools. With no helper capacity, grid evaluation remains serial. The opt-in invocation owns and drains all workers before returning or retrying. The legacy path only shuts down its pool and preserves prompt caller return on interruption or failure; its workers may finish afterward.

`ClassWeightGridSearch` constructs deterministic CV splits before scheduling. Helpers can run pairs 1–8 while the fold worker owns pair 0; the caller can also execute unclaimed queued tasks. FutureTask ensures each pair executes once. Every pair retains its three serial fits, and scores/failures are consumed by ordinal. Strict original-order tie selection remains intact. RNG consumption, fold membership, scaling reductions, convergence, confidence semantics, and output ordering are unchanged.

The existing `JavaPotOptions.maxWorkers` is the total training-worker parameter for this path. A caller can supply its allocated core count; JavaPot does not automatically claim the entire host. Twelve workers mean three outer workers plus up to nine helpers; 24 mean three plus 21. JVM GC/JIT and the calling thread are outside the active training-worker budget. Public CLI/API defaults still use the legacy path. With three folds there are at most 27 concurrent pair evaluations, so 24 is already close to that mechanism’s available parallelism. Performance at 24 is deferred to target hardware; exactness and worker-bound tests cover 24 on this host.

Earlier grid prototypes are superseded, rather than combined with the selected results. V2 preserves legacy public interruption behavior; V3 adds deterministic internal interruption/cancellation and recovery cleanup; the selected caller-reservation refinement guarantees each fold worker actually evaluates a pair. Their JARs, source snapshots, manifests and timing identities remain separate under `experiments/grid/` and `experiments/grid-caller-reservation/`. The first bounded full-suite attempt stopped at its testing-window deadline; the subsequent complete selected-prototype suite passed. That stopped attempt remains recorded in `bounded-full-test-command.json` and its log, rather than being presented as a completed suite. All preliminary timing arms remain in `all-arms.tsv`; they do not qualify the final ff4 artifact.
